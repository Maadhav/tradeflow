Tradeflow

Every loan decision is a CRE workflow.

listing

HTTP, in a TEE

Private credit review inside the enclave, priced with the Chainlink EUR/USD feed. Deploys the loan's ERC-3643 token.

lender

HTTP

KYC over Confidential HTTP. Instructs the on-ramp and rejects a rate more than 1% off the feed.

settlement

EVM log, HTTP

Pays the business when funded. Repayment only when bank and processor agree.

monitor

Cron

Reconciles the books, pauses funding on a mismatch, locks late loans.
