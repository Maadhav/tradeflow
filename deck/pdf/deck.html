One product, two sides.

Businesses see what they have been advanced, what buyers still owe and every document, and request financing in minutes.

Investors see their positions, expected returns and what they have been paid, and pick new loans from the marketplace.

Trust without trusting us.

  • The buyer confirms every document before it can be financed, and no document can be financed twice.
  • Credit is reviewed privately: a business's financial data is never shown to investors or operators.
  • Repayment is confirmed by two independent sources before investors are paid.
  • Reserves are checked continuously. If the books disagree, new funding stops automatically.
  • Late loans are flagged and the business cannot borrow again until it pays.

The bank booked money that never arrived, so funding paused on its own.

Why it has to be onchain.

Capital from anywhere

A bank transfer in Madrid and USDC from a wallet in Singapore fund the same loan and hold the same note.

Compliant by design

Each loan is a permissioned security token (ERC-3643). Only verified investors can hold or trade it.

Every decision on record

Listings, payouts, repayments and reserve checks are written onchain, so anyone can audit the book.

Rules that run themselves

Repayments settle automatically, late loans lock, and funding pauses when reserves do not match.

See it work.

A business requests financing, its buyer confirms, two investors fund it, the business is paid, the buyer pays, and both investors are repaid.

Built in 36 hours, running end to end.

  • The full lifecycle runs on Ethereum Sepolia: listing, funding by bank and by wallet, payout, repayment, late loans. Every step is a real transaction.
  • 20 contract tests and an end-to-end test of 15 checks covering both portals.
  • Under the hood: Chainlink CRE automates every off-chain decision, including a confidential credit review; loans are ERC-3643 tokens; prices come from Chainlink data feeds.
