One product, two sides.

Businesses: advances, amounts owed by buyers, documents.

Investors: positions, returns, payouts and the marketplace.

How investors are protected.

  • The buyer confirms every document. No document is financed twice.
  • Credit is reviewed privately. Investors never see a business's financials.
  • Two independent sources confirm each repayment.
  • Reserves are checked continuously. A mismatch pauses funding.
  • Late loans lock the business until it pays.

Funding paused: the bank booked money that never arrived.

Why onchain.

One loan, any money

Bank transfers and USDC fund the same loan and hold the same note.

Permissioned tokens

Each loan is an ERC-3643 token. Only verified investors can hold it.

Auditable

Listings, payouts, repayments and reserve checks are all onchain.

Automatic

Repayments settle, late loans lock and funding pauses on a reserve mismatch.
