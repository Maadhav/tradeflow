Chainlink CRE makes every off-chain decision.

Listing

HTTP trigger, confidential TEE handler, EUR/USD Data Feed

Is the document real, confirmed and creditworthy? Then list the loan and issue its token.

Lender

Confidential HTTP, Vault DON secret, Data Feed check

Is the lender verified? Did the bank deposit really arrive at a fair rate?

Settlement

EVM log triggers, two-source consensus

Pay the business once funded. Mark it repaid only when the bank and the processor agree.

Monitor

Cron trigger, three-way reconciliation

Late loan? Freeze the business and pause its token. Books out of balance? Pause all funding.

Four workflows, seven handlers. Each decision lands onchain as a signed report through the CRE forwarder.

Confidential by design.

Stays in the enclave (handlerInTee, AWS Nitro)

  • Revenue, open debts, payment history
  • Document registry and sanctions results
  • The API secret

Only the verdict leaves

  • Grade, APR, advance and a document hash

The same run in the CRE CLI: the handler requests AWS Nitro, the simulator runs it locally and says so, then the signed report lands on Ethereum Sepolia.

  • One T-REX token per loan, deployed by the market when the listing report lands.
  • CRE is the trusted claim issuer: a lender is verified only when the KYC workflow's report writes the claim.
  • Only verified wallets can hold or receive notes, whether they lent by bank transfer or in USDC.
  • A late loan's token is paused onchain until it is repaid.

Lenders do not take our word for it.

  • Every decision is a signed report written onchain, with the evidence that produced it.
  • Repayment needs two independent sources to agree: the collection bank and the payment processor.
  • Reserves are reconciled across bank books, on-ramp mints and onchain credits. A mismatch pauses funding onchain.
  • Double financing is blocked: a document's hash can be listed only once.

The bank booked a deposit that never reached the chain: the monitor paused new funding onchain.

It runs on Ethereum Sepolia.

  • Every step of the loan lifecycle is a cre workflow simulate --broadcast run that writes a real Sepolia transaction.
  • 20 contract tests, and an end-to-end test of the full lifecycle with 14 checks.
  • Every run's terminal output and transaction in the repo's evidence folder.
